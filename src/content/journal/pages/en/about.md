Hi, I'm Cesar. I'm a software engineer, and I study psychology.

This journal is where both sides meet. I write about:

- **What I build**: tools, apps and experiments, like [[yuyay]] and [[pawlypaint]].
- **What I study**: notes from psychology class, written by someone who thinks in systems.
- **What I play**: the games that keep me up at night, and why they work.
- **What I'd like to film**: ideas for videos, from the first note to published.

## How I work
I develop alone, a few hours a week, with help from Claude Code. Everything here is built to be cheap, private and easy to keep going.
