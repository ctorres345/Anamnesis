Yuyay is a free library and educational simulator of psychological tests for students and professionals in Latin America, with Peru as the pilot country.

It runs entirely in the browser: no account, no backend and no stored data. Every test shows its license, where it comes from and which countries have an official version. The simulator only appears when the license allows it.

> Each result says clearly that it is an educational simulation without clinical validity.

You can try it at [yuyay.cjtech.dev](https://yuyay.cjtech.dev).
